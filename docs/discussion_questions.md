# Discussion Questions for Class

## Section A: Understanding the Framework

1. **Lowi argues that "policies determine politics."** Using two examples from the 2024-25 review — one distributive and one redistributive — explain how the *type* of policy shaped the political dynamics around its passage.

2. **Why is agriculture in India almost entirely distributive?** What would a regulatory or redistributive agricultural policy look like, and why is it politically difficult to enact?

3. **The Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) was classified as redistributive.** Some might argue it is distributive (a benefit to government employees funded by taxpayers). Which classification do you find more convincing, and why?

---

## Section B: Applying the Framework to India

4. **India's financial sector generated the most regulatory policies in 2024-25.** Is this evidence of regulatory maturity or regulatory overreach? How would Lowi's framework help us distinguish between the two?

5. **The Simultaneous Elections Bill is constituent.** But critics argue it would fundamentally alter redistributive dynamics by reducing state-level accountability. How does a single policy span multiple Lowi categories, and what does this mean for the framework's analytical power?

6. **Compare the political dynamics around MSP (distributive) and Ayushman Bharat expansion (redistributive).** Which generated more political conflict? Does this align with Lowi's predictions?

---

## Section C: Critical Evaluation

7. **Lowi developed his framework for American politics.** Identify three ways in which India's political context (coalition politics, caste dynamics, centre-state relations) challenges or modifies the framework.

8. **Some policies resist clean classification.** Pick two "borderline" policies from the dataset and argue for alternative classifications. What does this tell us about the framework's limitations?

9. **James Q. Wilson extended Lowi by asking: who bears the costs and who gets the benefits?** Apply Wilson's cost-benefit matrix to three policies from the 2024-25 review. Does Wilson's framework add analytical value over Lowi's?

10. **If you were advising the government on the next year's policy agenda**, which Lowi category would you recommend prioritising, and why? Consider both political feasibility and development impact.

---

## Section D: Data Exercise

11. **Using the CSV dataset**, create a 2×2 matrix of sector × category and identify which cells are empty. What does the *absence* of certain policy types in certain sectors tell us about India's policy landscape?

12. **The dataset includes outlay figures for distributive policies.** Rank the top 10 by fiscal commitment. Is there a correlation between outlay size and political salience? How does this relate to Lowi's predictions about distributive politics?

---

## Suggested Format

- Groups of 4-5 students
- Each group picks 2 questions from different sections
- 10-minute presentation + 5-minute Q&A
- Submit a 2-page write-up per question
