# 🏛️ India's Annual Policy Review 2024-25 — Lowi's Framework Classification

> **Classifying 75+ Indian policy actions (April 2024 – March 2025) using Theodore Lowi's typology of public policy.**

This repository accompanies a group study project for policy analysis. All policies are sourced from **PRS Legislative Research's Annual Policy Review (April 2024 – March 2025)**.

---

## 📖 What is Lowi's Framework?

Theodore Lowi (1964, 1972) argued that **policies create politics** — not the other way around. He identified four arenas, each with its own political dynamics:

| Type | What it does | Politics it creates | Example from India 2024-25 |
|------|-------------|--------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Distributive** | Allocates benefits to specific groups; costs diffused across taxpayers | Log-rolling, low conflict | MSP hikes, PMGSY-IV rural roads |
| **Redistributive** | Transfers resources from one broad class to another | Class-based, ideological conflict | Unified Pension Scheme, Ayushman Bharat 70+ expansion |
| **Regulatory** | Imposes rules/constraints on private conduct | Interest-group bargaining | SEBI market-abuse norms, Bharatiya Vayuyan Vidheyak |
| **Constituent** | Creates or restructures government institutions | Institutional/procedural debate | Simultaneous Elections Bill, Disaster Management restructuring |

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 1. Regulatory dominance
India's 2024-25 policy year was heavily regulatory — RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, CERC, and TRAI all issued major frameworks. This reflects the maturing of India's financial and infrastructure markets.

### 2. Distributive spending remains the backbone
Agriculture (MSP, fertiliser subsidies, natural farming), infrastructure (roads, offshore wind, solar villages), and social schemes (tribal development, housing) consumed the bulk of fiscal outlays.

### 3. Redistributive policies are few but high-impact
The Unified Pension Scheme, Ayushman Bharat senior citizen expansion, and PMAY Urban 2.0 represent significant cross-class transfers — and each generated substantial political debate.

### 4. Constituent reforms signal structural ambition
The Simultaneous Elections Bill, shipping sector restructuring (Indian Ports Bill, Maritime State Development Council), and Supreme Court rulings on federalism (mineral taxation, arbitration) all reshape the architecture of governance itself.
